Your plant's hydration level is the average Volumetric Water Content (VWC) of 4 soil moisture sensors. VWC is measured by dividing the volume of water by the total volume of the soil. The higher the percentage, the more hydrated your plant is. Please research your plant's ideal VWC to ensure it is healthy (look up "x plant VWC"). If the VWC is too low, your plant may be at risk of dying. If the VWC is too high, your plant may be at risk of root rot.
